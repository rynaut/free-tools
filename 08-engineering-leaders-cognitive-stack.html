MODULE 01 · THE FIVE HABITS

Start ridiculously small

Each habit below is designed as an if-then recipe: anchored to a routine you already have, scaled so small it takes under two minutes, and paired with a moment of acknowledgment. This is how habits actually stick — not through willpower, but through design.

GROUNDED IN · Fogg Behavior Model (Stanford Behavior Design Lab) · Implementation intentions (Gollwitzer) · Lally et al. (2010): automaticity forms over ~59–66 days, not 21 · Identity-based habits (Clear)
WHY TINY WINS
A 2025 study found that leaders who began with minimal viable habits and scaled up gradually were 2.7× more likely to maintain them long-term than those who started ambitious. Lower activation energy means more repetitions — and every repetition of anchor → behavior → acknowledgment builds the neural pathway. Tiny habits win because they actually happen.

The Peak Window Protocol

Once the tiny habits are landing, this is the structure they build toward. Your brain has a 90-minute peak cognitive window in the morning — this protocol protects it and sequences your day around your biology, not your calendar.

MODULE 03 · DECISION ARCHITECTURE

The Decision Firewall

Engineering leaders treat all decisions at the same cognitive cost. They don't have the same cost. Tier them — so your highest-quality cognitive resource goes only where it matters.

GROUNDED IN · Decision fatigue / ego depletion (Baumeister, 2011) · Prefrontal cortex resource limits
TIER 1 · STRATEGIC

Peak-window only. Never in meetings.

Architecture choicesTechnology betsHiringBuild vs buy
Rule: Decided only during your morning peak window. Sleep-on-it rule for anything with 3+ month impact. Meetings are for alignment, not deciding. Write your reasoning before you commit.
TIER 2 · TACTICAL

24-hour rule. Async first.

Technical approachProcess changesTool evaluationScope calls
Rule: Default to async, give yourself 24 hours unless truly time-critical. Most Tier-2 calls can be delegated to a senior with the right framing from you. Reserve your attention for the ones only you can make.
TIER 3 · OPERATIONAL

Batch. Time-box 3 min. Default yes.

PR approvalsAccess requestsCalendar invitesSlack questions
Rule: Batch into one midday block. Time-box each at 3 minutes; if longer, re-tier it. Default to "yes unless a strong reason not to." Twenty scattered Tier-3 decisions deplete the same resource as twenty Tier-1 ones.
THE DIAGNOSTIC
When a decision lands, ask: "What's the cost if this is wrong?" Hours → Tier 3. Days–weeks → Tier 2. Months–years → Tier 1. The Module 01 habit "Tier one decision before lunch" trains this until it's automatic.
